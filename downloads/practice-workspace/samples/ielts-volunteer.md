# 议论文｜论证、语法与立场

> 原创虚构教学材料；不是实际学员、品牌或企业成果。

## 题目 / 岗位
原创 Task 2 练习：Some people think university students should be required to do community volunteering. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

## 原始材料
Some people believe university students should be required to do community volunteering. I agree that volunteering can be useful, but I do not think it should be compulsory for every student. Universities should make these activities easy to join and give students choices about when to participate.

Volunteering is good for students because it has many benefits. Students meet people outside their courses and learn to work with others. For example, helping at a local reading club could give a student experience of explaining an idea to a child. They must listen carefully and change their explanation when the child does not understand. This is a useful skill in group projects and later at work.

However, a compulsory programme can create difficulties. Some students has paid jobs after class. They depend on that income to cover rent or transport. Adding several hours of unpaid work every week may leave them with less time for study or rest. A university should not assume that every student has the same amount of free time. Flexible weekend activities or short projects during holidays would be easier for many students to attend.

There is also a practical question about the organisations receiving help. A charity may need people who return regularly and understand its work. Sending students who only want to finish a requirement may not meet that need. Universities could instead match interested students with organisations and explain the responsibilities before they begin.

In conclusion, volunteering can help students and local communities. It should therefore be required for all students. Universities should also provide flexible opportunities and information so that students can make informed choices.
