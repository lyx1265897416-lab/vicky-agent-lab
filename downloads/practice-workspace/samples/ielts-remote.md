# 议论文｜空泛词与段落功能

> 原创虚构教学材料；不是实际学员、品牌或企业成果。

## 题目 / 岗位
原创 Task 2 练习：Working from home is better than working in an office. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

## 原始材料
Many people believe that working from home is better than working in an office. I think a flexible arrangement can be useful, but the best option depends on the job and the employee. A policy that offers some choice is more reasonable than a single rule for every worker.

The main advantage of home working is that employees can avoid a daily journey. They may use that time for rest or family responsibilities. For someone with a quiet room and a stable internet connection, it may also be easier to concentrate on a long writing task. These benefits do not apply equally to everyone, however. A person sharing a small flat may struggle to find a suitable space.

Working at home is very good and very useful for many workers. Good communication is very important and helps people work very well. Teams still need to coordinate their tasks and share information. A short scheduled meeting can help them agree on priorities, while written updates allow colleagues to read details at a convenient time. Too many meetings would remove some of the flexibility that home working is supposed to offer.

An office can be useful when a task requires equipment or close cooperation. New employees may also find it easier to ask questions when experienced colleagues are nearby. Employers could arrange office days for training or joint projects and allow independent tasks to be completed elsewhere. Such arrangements should be reviewed with employees rather than treated as permanent rules.

In conclusion, neither home nor office working is best in every situation. Employers should consider the work, the available space and the needs of the team. A flexible policy with clear communication can preserve useful contact while giving employees more control over their time.
