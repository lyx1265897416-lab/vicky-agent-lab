# 议论文｜合理表达不应硬改

> 原创虚构教学材料；不是实际学员、品牌或企业成果。

## 题目 / 岗位
原创 Task 2 练习：Cities should spend more on public transport than on new roads. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

## 原始材料
Some people argue that cities should spend more on buses and trains than on building new roads. I generally agree, although road maintenance should remain part of a transport budget. Public transport can help people who cannot drive and can use limited urban space more efficiently.

A reliable bus service gives residents more choice. A person without a car can travel to a job interview or a medical appointment. This is particularly important when jobs and services are far from affordable housing. However, a cheap ticket is not enough on its own. If a bus arrives only once an hour, people may still need another way to travel. Investment should therefore include both reasonable fares and a useful timetable.

Trains and buses can also carry many passengers in a relatively small amount of road space. This can reduce the need to park cars near busy centres. I do not think every passenger will stop driving, because some journeys involve several destinations or heavy equipment. Nevertheless, a frequent service on popular routes may make public transport a practical option for more people.

New roads can be helpful in some situations. A growing neighbourhood may need a safe connection to a hospital, and existing roads need repair. These projects should be assessed individually rather than rejected simply because public transport is important. The government should publish the expected benefits and explain which users each project will serve.

In conclusion, cities should give public transport a larger share of new investment while maintaining essential roads. Decisions should reflect the routes people actually need, and the results should be checked after a service begins. This approach offers a more balanced response than treating every road or every bus route as equally useful.
